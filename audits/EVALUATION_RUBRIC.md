# Fixed simulated evaluation rubric

**Fixed before final scoring on 2026-11-03.** These scores are nonofficial
portfolio simulations, not admissions predictions or university assessments.
No category may receive credit for an unsupported claim.

| Category | Points | Full-credit standard |
|---|---:|---|
| Scientific project quality | 15 | Important bounded question, coherent design, useful result, limitations integrated. |
| Research originality | 12 | A nontrivial contribution beyond applying a standard model, including meaningful negative findings. |
| Statistical rigor | 16 | Dependence-aware validation, uncertainty, denominators, sensitivity analysis, and restrained inference. |
| Reproducibility | 14 | Code, fixed inputs, hashes, tests, manifests, commands, and preservation of historical outputs. |
| Technical depth | 13 | Sound data pipeline, multiple validation regimes, model comparison, diagnostics, and traceable implementation. |
| Evidence of personal contribution | 10 | Specific human-authenticated decisions and work, with transparent AI boundaries. |
| Communication quality | 10 | Accurate first impression, readable figures, consistent paper/README/brief, easy navigation. |
| Admissions relevance | 10 | Clear evidence of mathematical/AI judgment and readiness for the named undergraduate programme. |
| **Total** | **100** | |

## Perspective rules

- The **Scientific Auditor** emphasizes correctness and independence; admissions
  polish cannot compensate for unsupported science.
- The **École Polytechnique** evaluator emphasizes mathematical/statistical
  reasoning, abstraction, and capacity to defend choices.
- The **PSL IBSAI** evaluator emphasizes empirical AI research, interdisciplinary
  significance, model reliability, and responsible judgment.

## Score bands

- 90–100: exceptional and independently evidenced.
- 85–89: very strong, with limited clearly disclosed gaps.
- 75–84: strong but missing material evidence or depth.
- 60–74: promising, substantial weaknesses.
- Below 60: insufficient observable support.

External validation is not an automatic zero, but its absence caps scientific
independence claims. Unverified personal contribution cannot score above 5/10.
